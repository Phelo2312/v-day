Which green building rating system fits your project?

The right certification depends on your project, your owner and your market. Here is how the main systems differ, and where our team has worked with each.

LEED

Run by: USGBC and GBCI, used worldwide

The world's most widely used rating system. Points-based, with four levels: Certified, Silver, Gold and Platinum.

Certified projects: 9 certified, 7 in progress

WELL

Run by: International WELL Building Institute

Certifies buildings for occupant health and wellbeing, including air, water, light, thermal comfort and sound.

Team accredited

Mostadam

Run by: Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Housing

Saudi Arabia's national system for sustainable building performance.

Supported: energy modeling for compliance

Estidama Pearl

Run by: Abu Dhabi government

Abu Dhabi's Pearl Building Rating System, from 1 to 5 Pearls. One Pearl is mandatory for new buildings in Abu Dhabi.

Team accredited

GSAS

Run by: GORD, Qatar

The Global Sustainability Assessment System, used in Qatar and across the Gulf.

One team and one contract, from feasibility to certificate

Most certified projects involve several specialists, and information is lost between them. At WAHDJ one accountable team, under one agreement, covers every technical step.

  1. Feasibility. A free review of the certification pathways open to your project and the documents you already hold.
  2. Design. Energy, daylight and life-cycle models shape the design while changes are cheap.
  3. Construction. Submittal review, waste tracking, indoor air quality protection and site training.
  4. Commissioning and certification. Functional testing, handover documents and submission to the certifying body.

We do not build. Your contractor builds; we consult, model, verify and certify. Read more about how we work.

The tools and standards behind our numbers

We build energy models in eQuest and DesignBuilder, comparing a baseline building with your proposed design. Daylight analysis uses Rhino, Grasshopper and ClimateStudio. Life-cycle assessment uses the Athena Impact Estimator. Commissioning follows ASHRAE, ASTM and NIBS guidance.

Led by Radwa Maher Amin

Radwa Maher Amin founded WAHDJ in 2017 and is an executive member of the Egypt Green Building Council. Our team holds LEED AP, BREEAM AP, GSAS-CGP, WELL AP, EDGE Auditor, Estidama Pearl Qualified and TARSHEED credentials. Meet the team on our About page.
